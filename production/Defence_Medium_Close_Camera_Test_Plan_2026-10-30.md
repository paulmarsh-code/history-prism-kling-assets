## Pilot rules
- Select short *unapproved* Defence-spoken dialogue from the approved tracker appropriate to each angle. Test medium and close for Witness, Jury, Judge; test Prosecution-facing shots ONLY if a genuine script line warrants addressing Prosecution. Do not invent a recipient.
- Mix shot sizes with editorial purpose: medium for orientation/hand gestures, close for key challenges and emphasis. Never force an alternation if the scene would suffer.
- Kling 4.0 Omni Reference where both image and video are available. Image controls identity/geometry. Video controls restrained performance style only, **not** its dialogue, exact gestures, hand placements or timing. New performance for each line.
- Preserve exactly: "Precise English speech, quiet realistic mouth motions" and "subtle natural mouth movement with accurate lip sync". Allow natural full jaw motion.
- Explicitly specify starting pose, ONE fresh natural hand/face action, no book contact unless intended, and ending pose; no sideways shuffle or gurning.
- Duration matched closely to words at approximately 125–145 wpm, smallest supported second setting that fits, avoiding filler, repeated speech and trailing pauses. Spell dates in words.
- Record dialogue accuracy, lip sync, face, voice, hand/prop continuity, camera axis and approval separately.
- No large Defence generation batches until pilot references have been approved.
