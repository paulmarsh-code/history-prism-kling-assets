## Locked production baseline

Kling **4.0 Omni Reference**, 720p, 16:9, one output per job, generated audio ON, original reference sound OFF. Supply both the approved image and the camera-angle reference video from GitHub. Kling Omni may internally enable multi-shot; prompt for a single visually continuous locked shot. Video references are billed in addition to output duration. Do not generate any clip marked APPROVED. A successful generation is only READY FOR REVIEW, never approved by default.

**REFERENCE RULE — universal:** Image 1 is the authoritative appearance, costume, camera-axis, furniture and eyeline reference. Video 1 is strictly an example of performance restraint, established voice and continuity, not source dialogue or reusable movement. Create a *genuinely new performance* for the stated words. Do not replay or substitute any reference-video dialogue, hand movement, body sequence, lip movements or timing. Do not touch the book unless a clip specifically says so.

**LIP-SYNC LOCK — wording preserved verbatim from the successful takes:** “Precise English speech, quiet realistic mouth motions” and “subtle natural mouth movement with accurate lip sync”. These directives allow full natural jaw articulation required by speech. No gurning, excessive eyebrows, theatrical grimacing or shuffling. No repeated words, letter-spelling, extra narration, subtitles or music.

**REVIEW GATE:** Verify exact spoken words, natural lip synchronisation, new gestures (not replay), camera/eye direction, hands and props before approving. Do not regenerate on generation completion without a new review decision.
