## Production baseline
- Kling 4.0 Omni Reference, image + video only after the Defence images/videos are approved and published in GitHub. *All available Defence image filenames currently end in REVIEW and are NOT automatically approved.*
- 720p, one clip per generation, new audio enabled, original reference sound disabled. Do not assume the model's reference video is new footage or copy its gestures or existing words.
- **Exact mandatory phrases:** “Precise English speech, quiet realistic mouth motions” and “subtle natural mouth movement with accurate lip sync”. These retain full natural jaw and lip articulation.
- Duration is an estimate based on spoken dialogue at about 125–145 words/minute, not a padded round-number default. If an excerpt needs more than 30 seconds, split only after checking source line boundaries.
- Numeric years are written in words in the generation text, while the original exact script remains recorded separately. Double-check historical name pronunciation.
- Medium-to-close-up distribution varies by rhetorical importance and adjacent framing. Close-ups emphasize key challenges, evidence and closing points; medium shots retain natural hand gestures and orientation. Avoid consecutive identical angle/scale unless continuity requires it.
- **Needed GitHub assets:** named Defence medium and close PNG plus corresponding approved MP4 for each recipient (Judge, Witness, Jury, Prosecution). Add/confirm all before starting batch automation. The video must be used for style and continuity only.
- Review gate: finished ≠ approved. Exact words, pronunciation, lip sync, gesture freshness, framing and correct reference first. Approved takes must not be regenerated.
