# History Prism Kling — Written-out dates policy

Effective 29 October 2026. Applies to every future Kling spoken-dialogue prompt, including prompts already drafted but not submitted.

- Preserve the historically approved script wording in the authoritative production tracker; transform *spoken generation text* only.
- Replace numeric years with conventional spoken English words to prevent digits being read separately. Example: `1933` -> `nineteen thirty-three`; `1502` -> `fifteen oh two` only where that specific reading is directed/approved for the clip.
- Dates with day/month/year should likewise be written as they are meant to be spoken. Do not change the factual date or silently change sentence meaning.
- Apply the rule before submitting a Kling job; log the spoken text used and confirm exact pronunciation during human review.
- Keep the successful lip-sync directions unchanged: `Precise English speech, quiet realistic mouth motions` and `subtle natural mouth movement with accurate lip sync`.
- Use the approved camera-angle image and video references, with explicit fresh gestures and no reference replay.
- Do not regenerate any approved clip unless separately authorised.
